# Returns and refunds

- Delivered orders can be refunded within 30 days of delivery.
- Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be refunded; the customer can cancel after delivery or refuse the parcel.
- Refunds up to $50 are approved automatically. Larger refunds need approval from a human agent (usually within 1 business day).
- Refunds go back to the original payment method in 3-5 business days.
- Items must be unused or defective. Digital goods are not refundable.
