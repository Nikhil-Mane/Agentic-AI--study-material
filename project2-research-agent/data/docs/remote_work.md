# Remote work policy

Employees may work from home up to three days per week with manager approval. Teams agree their office days at the start of each quarter.

Fully remote arrangements need written approval from a department head and are reviewed every six months.

Employees working abroad for more than 14 days in a year must notify HR because of tax and visa rules. Working from another country is not allowed for longer than 30 days.

Home-office stipend: a one-time allowance of 400 USD for a desk, chair or monitor. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days.
