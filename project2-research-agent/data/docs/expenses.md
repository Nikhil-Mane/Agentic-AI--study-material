# Expense policy

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Flights must be booked in economy class through the company travel portal at least 14 days in advance. Hotel stays are capped at 220 USD per night.

Expense reports must be filed within 30 days of the spend. Reports older than 60 days are rejected.

Any single purchase above 500 USD needs prior written approval from a manager.
